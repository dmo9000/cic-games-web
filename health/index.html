Health & Safety

Photosensitive Seizure Warning

A very small percentage of people may experience a seizure when exposed to certain visual images, including flashing lights or patterns. Even people who have no history of seizures or epilepsy may have an undiagnosed condition that can cause photosensitive epileptic seizures while watching video games or other electronic media.

These seizures may have a variety of symptoms, including lightheadedness, altered vision, eye or face twitching, jerking or shaking of arms or legs, disorientation, confusion, or momentary loss of awareness.

If you experience any of these symptoms, stop playing immediately and consult a doctor.

Visual Effects in Core64

This game contains rapidly flickering colours, strobing text effects, and high-contrast colour transitions that may trigger seizures in photosensitive individuals.

The game also features continuous 3D motion with rotating and tumbling objects.

Recommendations

Play in a well-lit room. Take regular breaks. Do not play if you are tired or need sleep. If you or any family member has a history of seizures or epilepsy, consult a doctor before playing.
